# Homogeneous Vertical Field – Weak Foam Co-Injection

This case simulates a homogeneous 2D vertical porous medium with gravitational effects under two-phase (water–gas) co-injection with weak foam.

## How to Run

    chmod +x Allrun.sh
    ./Allrun.sh

## Physical Description

This case considers a two-phase (water–gas) flow in a **homogeneous vertical porous medium**, where water and gas are co-injected in the presence of weak foam.

The system is influenced by:
- gravitational forces due to density differences between phases  
- foam-induced gas mobility reduction  
- viscous flow behavior  

Gas, being lighter, tends to rise, while water moves downward, leading to gravity-driven segregation. The presence of foam introduces additional resistance to gas flow, although its effect is limited due to the **weak foam strength**.

The interaction between:
- gravity  
- phase mobility  
- weak foam effects  

leads to a flow regime where buoyancy plays a role, while foam provides moderate stabilization of the displacement.
