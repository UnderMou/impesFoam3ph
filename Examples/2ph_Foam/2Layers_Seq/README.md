This case simulates a two-phase (water–gas) flow in a porous medium with **vertical permeability heterogeneity**, where regions of different permeability are arranged sequentially along the main flow direction.

## Physical Description

This case considers a two-phase (water–gas) flow through a porous medium with **vertical permeability heterogeneity**, where regions of distinct permeability are arranged sequentially along the main flow direction.

Water and gas are co-injected into the domain, and the flow evolves as the fluids encounter zones with different permeability.
